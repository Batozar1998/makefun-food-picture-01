# Build notes — food-picture-01

## Dish chosen
A single smash burger, close-up and full-frame. Chosen because it is the
most universally mouth-watering hero dish: melted cheese, a juicy seared
patty, fresh vegetables, and warm light — it reads instantly as delicious,
even at small size. One dish only, no text, no hands, so nothing competes
with the food.

## Candidates tried
Three burger shots were generated (close-up three-quarter, slightly high
angle on a wooden board, straight-on hero). The close-up three-quarter shot
won: its single cheese cascade is the most iconic appetite trigger, every
layer is distinct (glossy sesame bun, lettuce, tomato, pickles, crispy
smashed patty), and steam is visible. The straight-on hero shot was
dramatic but its square crop cut the bottom bun; the board shot was
messiest, with cheese and sauce overwhelming the layers.

## Final image
`entry.png` — 1024x1024, JPEG bytes (242,467 B, under the 1 MiB cap),
centre square crop of the close-up shot, resized with Lanczos. Re-read
after compression: no text, no artefacts, no broken shapes; the burger is
whole and the cheese drip is clean.
