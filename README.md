# The Perfect Smash Burger

One hero dish, shot like a food magazine cover: a smash burger with a glossy
sesame brioche bun, a thick flame-seared beef patty with crispy lace edges,
and a ribbon of melted cheddar cascading down the side. Fresh lettuce, ripe
tomato and pickles peek out under the cheese; a wisp of steam rises in warm
golden light against a dark, clean background. No text, no hands — just the
burger, full-frame and impossible to ignore.

The shot is built to make you hungry at first glance: the cheese drip is
the focal point, the patty's sear and the bun's shine give texture you can
almost taste, and the warm light leads the eye straight to the food. It
reads clearly even at small size — the burger and its cheese cascade are
unmistakable.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output/entry.png`). Running it twice writes identical bytes.
